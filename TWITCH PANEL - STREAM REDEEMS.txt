STREAM REDEEMS TWITCH PANEL
===========================

assets/twitch-panels/stream-redeems-panel.png

Link this panel to:
https://kiwi-vdb.github.io/The-Nest/redeems.html

HOW TO ADD IT
-------------
1. Open your Twitch channel page.
2. Open the About section beneath the stream.
3. Turn on Edit Panels.
4. Add a new image panel.
5. Upload stream-redeems-panel.png.
6. Paste the link above into the panel's Image Links To box.
7. Save the panel and turn Edit Panels off.

The supplied image is 320 x 120 pixels and under 1 MB, ready for Twitch.
